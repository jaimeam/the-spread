# The Spread - Friday, 27 November 2026

Houthi strikes reached Saudi Arabia's Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan. Brent climbed back near $96 as supply fears outweighed this month's surplus talk. A US-led group also signed a deal for Lukoil's foreign assets, and Europe's gas draw is getting faster in the cold.

## Houthis strike Jeddah, Jazan

Yemen's Houthis struck Saudi Arabia's Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan, and the exchange of fire with the kingdom shows no sign of easing [Rigzone]. This follows Wednesday's attack on Ras Tanura. The Houthis now say that strike caused fires. Saudi Arabia had said drones were intercepted there [OilPrice.com].

Damage at either coast isn't reported yet. A Saudi or Aramco statement on loadings will show whether exports were touched.

Prices moved anyway. Brent for January traded at $95.90/bbl at noon ET Thursday, up 1.3%. It closed at $89.37 on Monday [OilPrice.com]. WTI was at $84.65 [OilPrice.com], after touching $79.07 earlier in the week [OilPrice.com].

Standard Chartered argues the market needs a fresh shock to fall further. That is the bank's view [OilPrice.com].

The Red Sea coast is Saudi Arabia's bypass for barrels that would otherwise transit Hormuz. Attacks on its ports put that outlet in play alongside the Gulf. A possible knock-on is higher war-risk premiums on Red Sea liftings.

## US group signs for Lukoil abroad

An Eldridge-led consortium that includes the US DFC has agreed to buy Lukoil International [World Oil]. The portfolio covers about 350,000 boe/d of production in 11 countries and three European refineries [World Oil]. On Thursday this rested on Reuters reporting. The buyers have now announced it [Rigzone]. Lukoil's side isn't reported.

The deal is a share swap and still needs OFAC approval [World Oil]. If it closes, the three European refineries move from sanctioned Russian ownership to US-allied hands.

Sources: [World Oil](https://www.worldoil.com/news/2026/11/26/u-s-led-consortium-agrees-to-acquire-lukoil-s-international-portfolio-in-share-swap-deal/) · [Rigzone](https://www.rigzone.com/news/eldridge_consortium_pens_deal_to_acquire_lukoil_international-26-nov-2026-185176-article/?rss=true)

## Hormuz transits rose as attacks mounted

From 1 to 19 November, the seven-day average of crude, fuel and LNG tankers crossing Hormuz was above 26 a day [Vortexa via Bloomberg via OilPrice.com]. Earlier in the war it was below 25 on at least two-thirds of days [Vortexa via Bloomberg via OilPrice.com]. This came while attacks and mine incidents ran at their highest of the war [gCaptain].

The IRGC Navy says an LNG carrier hit a mine and exploded. It says the ship had switched off its tracking and used a route Tehran deems illegal [IRGC via OilPrice.com]. The ship is unnamed and the claim is unconfirmed.

ADNOC's tanker restriction now has more detail. A Petroleum Ports Authority notice bars tankers carrying crude, products, liquefied gases and chemicals from moving near the main export facilities [Splash247].

## Iranian hardliners push a convoy

Commentators close to the IRGC are pushing for a declared tanker convoy to sail through the US blockade. They pair it with threats against Gulf neighbours' oil infrastructure if US forces fire on Iranian ships [OilPrice.com]. Iran's crude and condensate revenue is projected to reach zero by mid-December [OilPrice.com].

This is advocacy, not a government decision. Kpler's 19 November note saw signs Iran may test the blockade with small tankers, but no breakout in exports [Kpler].

## OPEC+ leans toward a rollover

Two delegates say OPEC+ is weighing a rollover of current quotas into January, citing uncertainty from the Iran war [Rigzone]. Iraq and Kazakhstan both want higher capacity assessments. A rollover would push those fights past Sunday's meeting. Nothing is decided.

Sources: [Rigzone](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/opec_mulls_sticking_with_current_quotas-26-nov-2026-185177-article/?rss=true)

## EU gas draw quickens in the cold

EU storage was 67.2% full on 25 November, down 0.63 points in a day [GIE AGSI]. The 23 November draw was 0.50 points [GIE AGSI].

Gasunie wants the Netherlands to build a strategic gas reserve. It says there is no immediate shortage risk [OilPrice.com]. In the UK, NESO flagged a margin shortfall of more than 2.7 GW for Thursday afternoon and evening, the third tight day running [NESO via OilPrice.com].

Egypt may add supply. An LNG carrier off Idku points to a possible export restart after nine months [Kpler].
