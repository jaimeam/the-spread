Turning to gas. Equinor shut its Visund platform in the Norwegian North Sea on Wednesday after a fire in an electrical switchboard room. According to NRK, Norway's public broadcaster, all of the field's export capacity is off the market from Thursday. That's just over thirteen million cubic metres a day, and no restart date has been reported.

The timing is awkward for European buyers. EU storage was already drawing, down about a quarter of a point on the third of November, according to Gas Infrastructure Europe.
